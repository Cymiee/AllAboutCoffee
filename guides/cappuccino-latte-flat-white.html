Cappuccino vs Latte vs Flat White

Three drinks, the same two ingredients. The difference is all in the milk.

On this page

    Same ingredients, different ratios

    All three are espresso and steamed milk. Nothing else. What separates them is how much milk, and critically, what texture that milk has been steamed to.

    Once you can see that, the menu stops being intimidating — you are really choosing a milk-to-espresso ratio and a foam thickness.

    It is really about the foam

    A cappuccino is built on a thick, airy foam cap. A latte uses more milk with only a thin layer of microfoam. A flat white sits in between: less milk than a latte, and the foam is folded through the drink rather than sitting on top.

    • Cappuccino — roughly equal espresso, milk and foam; thick cap
    • Latte — the most milk; thin layer of microfoam
    • Flat white — less milk than a latte; velvety, foam folded in

    How to order what you actually want

    If you want to taste the coffee most clearly, a flat white gives you the highest espresso-to-milk ratio of the three. If you want it mildest and most comforting, order the latte.
