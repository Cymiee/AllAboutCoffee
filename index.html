From Seed to Cup

The long road a coffee bean travels before it reaches your mug.

  1. 01

    Seed

    A coffee “bean” is really the seed of a cherry. Planted in shaded nurseries, it takes about two months to sprout.

    ~2 months to sprout
  2. 02

    Tree

    Seedlings are moved out to the farm, often high up in the tropics, and grow for years before they bear any fruit.

    3–4 years to first fruit
  3. 03

    Cherry

    White, jasmine-scented blossoms turn into cherries that slowly ripen from green to deep red.

    ~8 months to ripen
  4. 04

    Harvest

    Ripe cherries are picked — often by hand, in several passes, because they don’t all ripen at once.

    picked as they ripen
  5. 05

    Processing

    The fruit has to come off the seed. Washed, natural and honey methods each leave their own mark on the flavour.

    washed · natural · honey
  6. 06

    Dry & mill

    Beans are dried in the sun, then hulled out of their papery parchment and sorted. Now they’re “green coffee”, ready to ship.

    dried to ~11% moisture
  7. 07

    Roasting

    Heat turns the green bean brown: sugars caramelise, aromas develop, and the beans audibly crack.

    first crack ~196°C
  8. 08

    Grinding

    Grind size is matched to the brew — coarse for a French press, fine for espresso. Grind just before brewing.

    coarse → fine
  9. 09

    Brewing

    Hot water pulls the flavour out of the grounds, and months of work end up in the cup in your hands.

    your cup

Coffee Drinks

Six classics — what they are and what sets them apart.

Pure. Intense. Essential.

Espresso

Hot water forced through finely-ground coffee under pressure. The foundation every café drink is built on.

~2 ozStrongestNo milk

Espresso, opened up.

Americano

Espresso diluted with hot water — lighter body, same bold character and depth.

~8 ozMediumNo milk

Silky. Mellow. Familiar.

Latte

Espresso with generous steamed milk and a thin foam cap. Smooth and approachable.

~12 ozMildLots of milk

Foam, flavor, balance.

Cappuccino

Equal thirds espresso, steamed milk, and thick foam. Bolder and airier than a latte.

~6 ozMediumFoamy

Coffee meets chocolate.

Mocha

Espresso and steamed milk enriched with chocolate syrup. Dessert in a cup.

~12 ozSweetChocolate

Espresso, just marked.

Macchiato

A shot of espresso with just a small dollop of foam. Bold and unapologetically strong.

~3 ozStrongLittle milk
